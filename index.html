Start with one thing you already need to do

You don’t need to try all twenty. Pick something already on your to-do list: a meeting tomorrow, a decision you keep revisiting, or a goal you’ve been postponing. Tell your AI assistant your situation, explain what you’re trying to accomplish, and ask for the outputs on the matching card. Then review the result, correct what needs correcting, and put it into practice.

The most useful AI prompt is often the one that helps you finish something you’ve already been meaning to do.

A small challenge for this week
  1. 1Choose one application that matches a task already on your list.
  2. 2Open its prompt, add your real details, and ask your AI assistant for the outputs on the card.
  3. 3Evaluate the result. Did it save time, improve your preparation, surface another option, or make the next action easier?

If the result felt generic, add more context or name the exact output you need. For anything that affects your health, finances, or business, verify the facts before you act.
