Start with one thing you already need to do

You don’t need to try all twenty. Pick something already on your to-do list: a meeting tomorrow, a decision you keep revisiting, or a goal you’ve been postponing. Give your AI assistant the context, explain what you’re trying to accomplish, and ask for an output you can actually use. Then review the result, correct what needs correcting, and put it into practice.

The most useful AI prompt is often the one that helps you finish something you’ve already been meaning to do.

A small challenge for this week

  1. Choose one tile that matches a task or responsibility you already have.
  2. Copy the prompt and customize it with your actual circumstances.
  3. Evaluate the result. Did it save time, improve your preparation, surface another option, or make the next action easier?

If it worked, save the prompt. If the result felt generic, add more context or specify the output you need. For anything that affects your health, finances, or business, verify the facts before you act.
