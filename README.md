Most of us started using AI for simple things like drafting an email or summarizing a document. AI becomes far more useful when you give it a specific task, enough context, and an outcome you can act on. This playbook collects 20 practical applications across work, time, decisions, everyday life, and personal goals. Click any title below to open its full prompt, with an example and next steps, ready to copy.

## How to use it

1. Pick one application that matches something already on your list this week.
2. Open its prompt, replace each `[placeholder]` with your real details, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI assistant.
3. Review the result, correct what needs correcting, and verify any facts that affect money, health, or your business.
